Synthetic Pros (SynPros) specializes in repairing and refinishing a wide range of surfaces, including bathtubs, showers, sinks, vanity tops, appliances, porcelain, fiberglass, acrylic, cultured marble, stainless steel, and more. Our goal is to restore damaged surfaces to a showroom quality finish while helping customers avoid the high cost and inconvenience of replacement.

By choosing restoration over remodeling, homeowners, contractors, property managers, and businesses can save time and money while extending the life of existing fixtures. Whether repairing chips, cracks, scratches, stains, or everyday wear and tear, SynPros delivers professional results that improve the appearance and value of any property.

Proudly serving the Greater Sacramento area, Fresno, and now Tennessee, SynPros is committed to providing quality craftsmanship, reliable service, and lasting results that make old surfaces look new again.
